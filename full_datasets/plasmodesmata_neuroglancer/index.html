Plasmodesmata - Whole-Dataset Results

Per-dataset Neuroglancer states overlaying the final plasmodesmata and cell segmentations on raw EM, plus download links to the per-dataset measurement CSVs produced by cellmap-analyze. Modeled on nuclear_pores_dacapo/full_datasets/create_np_original_resolution_neuroglancer.py.

  • raw — EM, multiscale parent group.
  • plasmodesmata — post-MWS / postprocessed plasmodesmata instance segmentation. Resolved from /nrs/cellmap/data/<dataset>/<dataset>.zarr/recon-1/labels/inference/segmentations/pd when present (OpenOrganelle-style final location, currently the b datasets); otherwise from leaf-gall/<dataset>.zarr/plasmodesmata_cleaned (the non-b datasets that haven't been moved yet).
  • cell_fixed — proofread cell-mask segmentation. When precomputed multires meshes are available (8 nm datasets only) they are bundled into the same layer via a second source.

Downloads. Each row's CSVs come from /nrs/cellmap/ackermand/cellmap/analysisResults/leaf-gall/<dataset>/. cell_fixed.csv + plasmodesmata_lines_assigned_to_2_nearest_cells.csv are only produced for the 8 nm datasets (cell-aware analysis).
